You can resume paused projects in a couple of different ways depending on your Netlify team account details. A Netlify team account is paused when projects use up their monthly usage limit.

## How pauses work

When a Netlify team account uses up its monthly usage limit, all projects owned by that team enter a paused state until the start of the next billing cycle or until you increase your usage limits by upgrading your plan.

If you do nothing, your web projects remain paused until the start of the next billing cycle. At the start of your next billing cycle, your web projects will automatically resume and start accepting form submissions, web requests, and more.

If you have a credit-based plan, you can buy more credits by enabling [auto recharge](#buy-more-credits-with-auto-recharge) or by [buying one-time credit packs](#buy-one-time-add-on-credit-packs) to keep your projects active on the web.

When a project is paused, it can no longer receive the following:
- web requests
- form submissions
- other types of traffic

In addition, all deploys will pause, including Deploy Previews and branch deploys.

Your site or app visitors will find a message that says `Site not available` that mentions your site is paused.

## For credit-based pricing plans

If you have a credit-based pricing plan, you can unpause or resume your projects' online operation by [upgrading to a paid plan](#upgrade-to-a-paid-plan) or if you already have a paid plan you can buy more credits in [credit packs](#buy-one-time-add-on-credit-packs) or through [auto recharge](#buy-more-credits-with-auto-recharge).

### Upgrade to a paid plan

> **Note - On a free plan?:** The free plan does not allow you to purchase more credits.

If you have a free plan and you want to resume or unpause your projects, you only have these options:
1. Upgrade to a paid plan with more credits and the option to purchase more credits when you need them.
2. Wait until your monthly plan's credits refresh at the start of the next billing cycle.

As a Team Owner, to upgrade to a paid plan, such as the [Personal or Pro](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/) plan:

1. From your Netlify team dashboard, go to

2. Under **Plan details**, choose **Change plan**. If you already have a paid plan, such as the Personal plan, you'll find a button that says **Upgrade plan**.
3. Choose your desired plan and confirm the changes.

For support upgrading to plans beyond Pro, [reach out](https://www.netlify.com/contact/sales/) to learn more.

### Buy one-time add-on credit packs

> **Note - On a free plan?:** The free plan does not allow you to purchase more credits.

If you have a free plan and you want to resume or unpause your projects, you only have these options:
1. Upgrade to a paid plan with more credits and the option to purchase more credits when you need them.
2. Wait until your monthly plan's credits refresh at the start of the next billing cycle.

If you are on a paid plan, such as the Personal or Pro plan, you can buy more credits in packs at any time. Credits bought in credit packs roll over for future months.

Learn more about [buying credit packs](/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/buy-credit-packs/).

### Buy more credits with auto recharge

> **Note - On a free plan?:** The free plan does not allow you to purchase more credits.

If you have a free plan and you want to resume or unpause your projects, you only have these options:
1. Upgrade to a paid plan with more credits and the option to purchase more credits when you need them.
2. Wait until your monthly plan's credits refresh at the start of the next billing cycle.

If you are on a paid plan, such as the Personal or Pro plan, you can buy more credits to resume your web project using auto recharge.

You must be a Team Owner to buy more credits using auto recharge, and if you have a Pro plan, you can add additional Team Owners to your project as needed. Learn more about [adding Team Owners](/manage/accounts-and-billing/team-management/manage-team-members).

Here's how auto recharge works:
- Auto recharge keeps your web projects active on the web by automatically purchasing credits in small increments as needed by your projects
- You can turn it on or off as needed at any time (it's off by default)
- Extra credits purchased through auto recharge will roll over to your next billing cycle after your team account has used its monthly plan credits.

These are the rates that auto recharge uses to purchase more credits for your project as needed:
- **Personal plan**: 500 credits for $5
- **Pro plan**: 1500 credits for $10

Note that if you have a free plan, you cannot use auto recharge. You can only wait until the next billing cycle starts or upgrade your plan.

Learn more about [auto recharge](/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/configure-auto-recharge).

## For Legacy pricing plans

If you have a Legacy free pricing plan, you can upgrade your plan to a Credit-based pricing plan. You can also wait until the start of your next monthly billing cycle.

### Upgrade plan

As a Team Owner, to upgrade:

1. From your Netlify team dashboard, go to 
### NavigationPath Component:

Usage & billing
.
2. Under **Plan details**, choose **Change plan** or in the bottom left of your team dashboard choose **Upgrade**.
3. Choose your desired plan and confirm the changes.

Finally, you can open a new Netlify team account and choose a Credit-based pricing plan. Learn more about [how credits work](/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/how-credits-work/) or estimate your usage costs with our [Pricing estimation calculator](https://www.netlify.com/pricing/#calculator).
